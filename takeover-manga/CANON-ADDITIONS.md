# Manga-Originated Canon — Additions Ledger

*Everything the manga has invented that is **not** in `lore-master.txt`, with its founder
status. This file exists so approved additions stop being re-litigated, and so the owner has
one list to fold into the lore bible when convenient.*

**The lore bible remains the source of truth.** Nothing here overrides it; these are
extensions into space the bible leaves open. Where a bible section is cited, the addition
was built to sit inside it, not beside it.

**Status key:** ✅ APPROVED (founder-confirmed, treat as canon) · 🟡 PENDING (in use, awaiting
sign-off) · ⚪️ TEXTURE (benign staging detail, no lore weight, approve by silence)

## Standing rules this ledger enforces

1. **No numerals in art or text.** No CP figure, price, ratio or date-count is ever printed
   on a page. Quantities are wordless notch-ladders, ring segments and chevrons. Product/UI
   numbers are living product canon (§16 note) and the manga must never pin them down.
2. **Contested Archive and Shelved canon are never touched.** The Wraith, Novia / the Great
   Spark, Dana Banks' abilities and the Jinx = Joe Paradise identity are neither used nor
   hinted. The masked broadcaster stays a distinct third figure.
3. **Exact marks are composited, never generated** (Prime Law, `VISUAL-CONSISTENCY-STANDARD`).
4. Anything invented mid-production lands in this ledger the same day, with a status.
