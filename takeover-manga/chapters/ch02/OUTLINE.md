## The one-line pitch

The war hands you a gun for free, then sells you everything that keeps it alive.
Rae goes to the Armory to fix her bot and finds out who really owns the credit —
and by the time she gets home, the corp that poisoned her city is handing out
free food to her neighbors and they are *grateful*.

## Chapter thesis

**Ch.1 gave Rae a reason. Ch.2 gives her a price — and shows her the boot is
wearing a smile.** She ends the chapter poorer, harder, and more alone in her
anger than when she started, because everyone around her just got fed.
