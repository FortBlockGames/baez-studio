## P7 — THE ARMORY ⭐ THE CHAPTER'S ONE SPLASH
**Function:** the in-world outfitter to the war, revealed at cathedral scale.
**Panels: 1 (full-bleed splash). Text items: 3.**
**This is the only splash in the chapter. Nothing else on this plate competes with it.**

**ART.** Full-bleed vertical 2:3. Camera at floor level just inside the intake throat,
extreme wide-angle, one-point perspective, vanishing point slightly above the horizon so
the racks tower.

A **cathedral of racks**. Ten-tier gantry walls of **identical unpainted grey Basic
Soldiers** run left and right to a vanishing point that dissolves into black haze — thousands
of them, arms docked, optics dark, all the same, all the way down. Overhead gantry cranes on
rails move single units like organ pipes. Catwalks cross the void at four heights, with
requisition crews the size of rice grains on them. Dust in the light shafts. The scale
signal: **a full-size Soldier bot at mid-distance is one centimeter tall in frame.**

Far down the nave, small but unmistakable, the **only warm light in the building**: a raised
gallery of **armored-glass cases**, lit from beneath like a jeweler's window. Inside them,
spaced out with **empty cradles between them**, stand the **Primes** — heavier silhouettes,
sealed layered torsos where the Basic has an open oval cavity, and enlarged multi-ring
power cores glowing in concentric segments. *Most cradles in the gallery are empty. That
emptiness is the print cap, drawn.*

# VOICE GUIDE — DELTA (continuous with the ch.1 v2 guide)

*Ch.1's entries for **McCLAINE**, **RAE MARLO**, **SKULLFACE** and the **broadcast voice**
stand unchanged and are binding. Skullface and the broadcast voice do not appear this
chapter. The following are additions.*

**GUS FENNER (57, Merica City native, mutation-marked, still paying).**
He talks about money the way other people talk about weather: it's just what's outside.
**Never bitter out loud** — bitterness would let Rae dismiss him. Sentences are short and
land on nouns, not adjectives ("HANDS." / "IT'S A BANK."). He does not give advice; he gives
**arithmetic**, and lets her do what she wants with it. He calls her KID — not fondly, just
accurately. He never says "I'm sorry" and never says "don't." He uses the corporate
euphemism **"SLIGHT MUTATIONS"** in quotation marks, which is the closest he ever comes to
anger. **He is not a mentor who unlocks her potential — he is proof of the price**, and the
warmth in him is that he tells her anyway. His longest line in the chapter is twelve words.
Two hard rules: (1) he never asks Rae for anything; (2) he never tells her what to do.

**CHICKOWS BRAND VOICE (kiosk / convoy / jingle).**
Wholesome family-restaurant cheer laid over ruthless economic logic — canon §5. It is
**never sinister, never smug, never winking.** The attendants and the convoy PA genuinely
mean it; that is the horror. Rules: always address the customer by role and never by name
("PILOT!", "DISTRICT 12!"). Always exclaim. Always use FAMILY as both noun and verb-adjacent
warmth. **Never state a consequence** — when asked what happens if you lose, the voice
answers with reassurance, not information ("WE'LL STILL BE HERE FOR YOU. ALWAYS!"). Never
say "debt," "interest," "collateral," "default," or "seize"; say POINTS, VICTORIES, FILE,
FAMILY. The brand never threatens because the brand has never needed to. Jingle/PA copy is
one exclamation longer than it needs to be.
*Letterer:* BRAND VOICE balloons are **scalloped, tailless, Chickows red `#E02418`** and
should be typeset **slightly larger** than adjacent human balloons — the brand is always a
little louder than the person it's talking to.

**SYSTEM VOICE (gauntlet / Armory intake / transit PA / operation alerts).**
Bureaucratically pleasant, structurally indifferent. Terminal punctuation is clipped;
courtesy is bolted on at the end where it can do no good ("NON-FIELDABLE. REPAIR ESTIMATE
ATTACHED. THANK YOU, PILOT!"). Never emotional, never apologetic. **Never prints a number.**
It is the same institutional cheer as the corpo brands with the sugar removed.

**RAE — chapter-specific note.** Her ch.1 tell (repeating other people's words back flat
before deciding what she thinks) fires twice and only twice: 2.3 `"NON-FIELDABLE."` and
9.6 `"WON'T REACH IT."` Do not add a third. In Act Three she stops repeating and starts
answering (17.4) — that is the character movement of the chapter, and it must not be
diluted by an extra beat. **She never explains the chapter's thesis out loud on P18.**
