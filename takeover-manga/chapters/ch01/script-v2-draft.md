## VOICE GUIDE

**McCLAINE (narrator mascot).** Carnival barker crossed with a dry cowboy. He SELLS
the war like a midway attraction and undercuts it with deadpan ("THE WHOLE BEAUTIFUL
MESS"). Always addresses the reader as CITIZEN. Never neutral, never encyclopedia —
if a caption could open a textbook, it isn't his. Appears in person ONLY on the
narrator page; elsewhere his voice may carry a flavored caption (used once, P8).
He is a showman, not a liar: everything he says is true, just gleefully framed.

**RAE MARLO (19, arcade rat).** Short sentences. Dry, guarded, allergic to slogans.
Jokes are armor ("PUT IT ON MY TOMBSTONE"); when the armor drops she gets QUIETER,
not louder ("...NO." / "ACCEPTABLE."). Anti-corpo by lived experience, not ideology —
she argues with billboards using evidence. Never speechifies; her longest line in the
chapter is twelve words. Her tell: repeating other people's words back flat
(ACCEPTABLE / A BOT AND A REASON / best hands) before deciding what she thinks.

**SKULLFACE (Redd Hollis — never named on-page).** Magnetic, unhurried, certain.
A recruiter who talks like he has already recruited you and is waiting for you to
notice. Compliments are surveillance ("...BEST HANDS IN THE DISTRICT, HUH." — he
heard that from somewhere). "Any Means Necessary" energy lives in the calm, not in
threats: he never raises his voice, never monologues, never does villain camp.
Calls citizens CITIZEN — the same word the system uses, weaponized as an invitation.

**Broadcast voice (masked LoG pirate signal).** Steady, declarative, revolutionary
cadence. The ONLY voice permitted to say "400 YEARS UNDER THEIR HEEL." Speaker is
never identified beyond a masked figure (Jinx identity remains shelved — no hints).
