Why does human editorial taste matter in AI video?

Generators make shots. Editors make films. The difference is judgment: which take earns the cut, where the beat lands, when to hold on a face and when to get out.

Josh uses AI the way a cinematographer uses a lens: to reach shots that used to need a six-figure budget. Every frame is still chosen, timed, graded and scored by a person who has spent fifteen years learning what makes an audience feel something. The same discipline goes into AI business stacks: the tools are new, but the process mapping, quality control and client ownership come from years of shipping real work to real deadlines.

What is the Baez Studio method?

01

Story before tools

The brief, the audience and the single feeling the film must leave are decided before any model is opened.

02

Locked references

Characters, products and brand systems are locked as references so every generation stays on-model.

03

Finished like a commercial

Edit, grade, score and mix happen in Premiere, Resolve and After Effects, just as they would on a traditional spot.

04

Systems you own

Every workflow lives in client accounts, with documentation and training, so the studio is never a dependency.

05

Measured speed

About 60% faster VFX and post turnaround and up to 3x production speed, used to give clients more iterations, not fewer hours of care.

06

Honest labels

Spec work is labelled as spec, NDA work stays private, and AI use is disclosed the way each client and platform requires.
