Studio Content Engine

The workflow Baez Studio uses on its own marketing: one Google Sheet of content ideas becomes draft social posts, newsletter blocks and branded flyers, each waiting for approval. It is an internal build, shown as a blueprint.

Brief

Publish consistently across Instagram, X, LinkedIn and email without a full-time social manager.

Josh's role

Architecture, prompt library, automation build and SOPs.

Formats

Google Sheet source, n8n workflow, Docs templates, social queue.

Scene breakdown & summary
  1. Source: a Google Sheet with one row per idea (topic, pillar, asset link, target date, status).
  2. Trigger: status changes to "Ready" and the workflow starts.
  3. Drafting: a custom GPT with the studio voice guide writes platform-specific captions and a newsletter block.
  4. Design: a branded flyer template fills headline, date and image automatically.
  5. Approval: drafts land in a review tab; nothing posts until it is approved.
  6. Publish & log: approved items are queued and the sheet records links and dates.
