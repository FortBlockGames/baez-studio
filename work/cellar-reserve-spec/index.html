Cellar Reserve: brief to 4K master

A self-initiated spec ad for a premium wine label, made to show every stage of a Baez Studio production. It is not a paid client commission.

Spec Ad Showcase
Brief

Present a single bottle with the weight of a heritage brand: cellar light, dust, stone and time, without a location shoot.

Josh's role

Concept, storyboard, generative compositing, grade and finishing.

Formats

4K 16:9 hero frame set; 9:16 and 1:1 crops planned in the storyboard.

Before and after

Scene breakdown & summary
  1. Brief: bottle reference and mood pinned with shot list.
  2. Storyboard: hand-drawn frames with shot labels and a warm amber palette.
  3. Production: bottle silhouette locked as a reference plate so the label shape never drifts between generations.
  4. Compositing: cellar arches, barrels and volumetric light generated around the locked bottle.
  5. Finish: grade, dust particles and light beams balanced in Resolve for the 4K master.
