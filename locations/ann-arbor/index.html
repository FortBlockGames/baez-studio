What do Ann Arbor startups hire Baez Studio for?

Mostly the moments where a technical team has to make non-technical people believe: a raise, a launch or a first enterprise deal.

Raise

Investor pitch films

A 60–90 second film for the top of the deck and the demo-day screen. Founders preparing for accelerator demo days, including companies coming through Ann Arbor SPARK and SPARK East programs, use it to make the problem visceral before the first slide.

Explain

Technical explainers for spinouts

University of Michigan research spinouts often have a breakthrough that takes ten minutes to explain. Generative visuals show the molecule, the sensor or the battery at work in 45 seconds, with copy your scientists sign off on.

Ship

Remotion product demos for SaaS

B2B SaaS teams ship features weekly. Remotion templates turn each release note into a branded demo clip, so marketing keeps pace with engineering without booking an editor every sprint.

Sell

Sales & ops AI stacks

Lean teams wire their CRM to AI-drafted follow-ups, investor update emails and event flyers for local meetups and conferences, reclaiming founder hours for building.

Ann Arbor FAQ

Does Baez Studio meet clients in person in Ann Arbor?

Yes. Kickoffs, workshops and planning sessions can happen in person around Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County, while production and reviews run remotely.

Can you make a pitch film before our demo day?

Yes. A Signature Brand Film takes 14–21 business days and a Hook Pack-style teaser 7–10, so start about four weeks before the date and the schedule is built backwards from it.

Can you explain deep technical research visually?

Yes. Explainers for research-driven companies are scripted with your technical team, and every claim and visual is approved by them before it is rendered in 4K.
