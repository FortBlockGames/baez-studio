The Single Shot starts at $1,500: one polished video up to 30 seconds with original music, sound design, and two revision rounds, delivered in 5–7 business days. The Brand Film starts at $5,000: one 60–90 second hero film plus four short-form derivatives, the signature launch piece. The Retainer starts at $7,500/month for always-on production: up to 8 finished assets, ≈4 minutes of runtime.
How long does it take to produce?
Spark Pack: 3–5 business days. Hook Pack: 7–10 business days. Brand Film: 14–21 business days. Retainer clients get a first draft within 5 business days of brief lock, with same-week turnaround on urgent cuts. Launch dates are real constraints. If you have a hard deadline, say so upfront and we'll work the calendar backwards together.
What's the difference between an AI cinematic and traditional motion graphics?
Motion graphics rely on stock footage, vector animation, or 3D rendering, which means long timelines, large teams, and costs that scale with shot count. AI cinematic production lets a single director generate photoreal scenes, characters, and environments from prompts and references, then composite them with sound design and (sometimes) live action. The result is closer to a short film than a brand ad, at roughly a tenth of the time and cost of a traditional cinematic shoot.

What makes a strong launch video?
Three things, in under 60 seconds: what this is, why it matters, what the viewer should do next. Cinematic films work because they earn attention through tone and craft instead of asking for it. The best launch films sell the world the brand or project lives in, not the feature set. This is especially true for game and web3 launches. People back things they believe in. The film's job is to make them believe.
How many revisions are included?
Hook Pack and Brand Film each include three structured revision rounds: Direction (concept and structure), Refinement (shot-by-shot adjustments), and Polish (color, sound, final pass). Retainer projects include two revision rounds per asset. Revisions are baked into the timeline, never billed as extras.
What file formats and aspect ratios do you deliver?
Every project ships as a 4K master plus platform-ready exports: Meta, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, X/Twitter, LinkedIn. Vertical (9:16), square (1:1), and horizontal (16:9) cuts are included. Captioned versions are available on request, useful for sound-off autoplay environments and accessibility.
Is BAEZ a good fit for web3 launches and NFT drops?
Yes, web3 and crypto projects are a strong fit. Token launch trailers, NFT mint reveals, lore drops, and community films for crypto projects, DAOs, and on-chain studios. BAEZ understands launch-day timing pressure, the rhythms of an announcement thread, and how to ship inside calendar windows that don't move. If you have a mint date or token launch, say so upfront and the timeline gets built backwards from there.

You do. Full commercial usage rights to all finished deliverables. BAEZ retains the right to feature the work in its showreel and case studies unless we agree to a confidentiality arrangement in advance. If the project is under NDA or pre-launch, just flag it on the brief.
